Accepted varieties: American English spelling is accepted, provided you keep your spelling consistent throughout your piece—choose one variety (UK or US) and do not mix them.

Targeted Proofreading Pass (Self-Audit)
  • • Tense consistency: Past simple for historical actions (sought, annexed, expanded); present simple for your analytical argument and contemporary links (illustrates, reveals, persists).
  • • Spelling consistency (UK or US): American spelling is accepted, so pick one variety and stick to it throughout—check -ise / -isation vs. -ize / -ization (civilising / civilizing, decolonisation / decolonization, colonisation / colonization, industrialisation / industrialization, prioritise / prioritize, recognise / recognize, characterise / characterize) and -our vs. -or (favour / favor, behaviour / behavior, colour / color, endeavour / endeavor), without mixing UK and US forms in the same text.
  • • Subject–verb agreement: Check singular vs. plural subjects across long clauses (e.g. this shift shows vs. these policies show).
  • • Word count calibration: Trim wordy fillers or expand thin clauses so your draft sits cleanly around 180–220 words.
